Magical Shapes Encyclopedia

Learn the rules for each 3D shape and play with its magical interactive model below!

🧊

1. Cube

  • ✨ Must have exactly 6 identical squares.
  • 🙈 Cannot contain a 2x2 block of squares.
  • 📏 Cannot have 5 or more in a straight line.
Interactive 3D
🧱

2. Cuboid

  • ✨ Contains exactly 6 rectangles.
  • 👯‍♀️ Opposite faces in 3D must be identical.
  • 🛑 In the net, opposite faces never touch!
Interactive 3D
🎪

3. Prism

  • 👯‍♀️ Has two identical bases (e.g. triangles).
  • 📦 The side faces connecting them are rectangles.
  • 🔢 Number of rectangles = sides of the base.
Interactive 3D
🔺

4. Pyramid

  • ⭐ Has only one base (square, triangle).
  • 🏕️ Side faces are triangles meeting at the top.
  • 🌟 The net usually looks like a star or flower!
Interactive 3D
🛢️

5. Cylinder

  • 🔘 Consists of two circles (top & bottom).
  • 📜 The curved body unfolds into one rectangle.
  • 📏 Rectangle length = circumference of circle.
Interactive 3D
🍦

6. Cone

  • 🔘 Contains one circle (the base).
  • 🍕 Body unfolds into a circular sector (pizza slice).
  • 📏 Arc length = circumference of the base circle.
Interactive 3D
